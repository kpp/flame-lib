Simple model

Every block distributes 50 FLM in proportion to satoshis burned. The underlying smooth trend starts at 100 sat/FLM; the slope slider sets its change over 10,000 blocks, numbered 0–9,999. The four smooth curves share those endpoints; noise can move the actual endpoints. Your budget is split equally across your blocks, allowing fractional satoshis.

Noise is a seeded, mean-reverting Gaussian walk in log space, with roughly 100-block correlation. The multiplier is lognormal and positive, with mean 1: 30% means its standard deviation is 0.30, not a ±30% bound. The same random path is reused for every trend and strategy, and zero noise gives the smooth trend exactly.

The combined rate is the market rate plus your per-block burn divided by 50. Your reward is your burn divided by that combined rate. The average shown above is your total budget divided by your total FLM, not the arithmetic mean of block rates. This is a toy simulation, not Flame consensus or a price forecast.
